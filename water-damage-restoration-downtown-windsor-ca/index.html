Water Damage Restoration in Downtown Windsor, CA

Water in a condo, townhome or older house around the Town Green? Call dispatch and say which floor and which unit the water is coming from.

If water is spreading through a home or shop near the Windsor Town Green, call (707) 221-5171 and tell dispatch the street, the floor and where the water started. Windsor Water Damage Pros is based on Brooks Road in Windsor, and downtown sits inside the town we call home.

Downtown is the part of Windsor where housing is packed closest together. The Town of Windsor planning documents describe a downtown that mixes older single-family streets with attached homes and apartments above shops, and that mix shapes how water damage behaves here.

Why downtown water damage is different

The 2012 Windsor Station Area Downtown Specific Plan counted 988 housing units and about 2,800 residents in its planning area, at an average of 8.5 homes per acre. It describes Town Green Village, built in 2001, as the densest block at 16 units per acre. When homes share floors, ceilings and walls, a leak in one unit rarely stays in that unit.

A failed washer hose on a second floor can reach the ceiling below, the wall next to it and the corridor outside. That is why the first call should name the floor and unit, and why a restorer needs access to the neighbors as well as to the source.

Water damage services we handle downtown

Most calls begin with water extraction to take standing water off the floor. For a pipe that has split inside a wall, the burst pipe page explains what happens before the plumber arrives. Wet ceilings, which are common when a unit above has a failure, are covered on the ceiling page.

Drying is the longest stage. Air movers and dehumidifiers run for days, and readings are taken each visit so you can see when walls and floors are back to normal. The structural drying page describes what is measured and why a surface that feels dry can still hold water.

Before the crew gets there

Stop the source if it is safe

Close the shutoff under the fixture or the main valve. In a shared building, tell the owner or property manager so the building supply can be isolated.

Stay out of water near electricity

Do not stand in water to reach a panel, and keep people and pets away from wet outlets and ceilings that are sagging.

Photograph the damage

Take wide and close photos of wet walls, floors and belongings before anything is moved, and keep the pictures with your policy details.

Warn the neighbors

A knock on the door of the unit below can catch a second leak early, and it helps the restorer get access.

Insurance in condos and apartments

Who pays can depend on where the water started. A homeowner association master policy may cover the building while your own policy covers the inside of your unit and your belongings, and a renter's policy works differently again. Read your policy and ask your insurer or association, because we cannot tell you what will be covered.

What we can do is document the loss. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope of work describe what was wet and what was done, and many insurers ask for exactly that.

Where downtown sits in the weather picture

The Specific Plan maps show Windsor Creek, East Windsor Creek and Starr Creek around downtown. Mapping a creek is not the same as saying a street has flooded, and we are not claiming that any block downtown has. Sonoma County's January 2023 storms brought 5 to 20 inches of rain across the county and the Town proclaimed a local emergency, so heavy rain is a real topic for every Windsor homeowner.

Flood-zone status for the blocks around the Green was not verified for this page. Use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center to check a specific address, and ask the Town of Windsor if the result is unclear. Zone X means lower mapped risk and not zero risk.

Questions from downtown Windsor residents

Is emergency water removal available near the Windsor Town Green?

Yes. Call (707) 221-5171 any time and give the street, the floor and whether the water has stopped. Dispatch explains the next step on the call and does not promise an arrival time.

Can you work in an apartment or condo above another unit?

Yes, with access. Water that reached the unit below needs its own check, so tell dispatch about both units and contact the owner or association early.

Who pays when a leak from another unit damages mine?

It depends on the cause, the building and the policies involved. Keep photos and invoices, read your policy, and ask the insurer or association who handles it.

Do you work on shops and offices around the Green?

Yes. Commercial spaces get the same extraction and drying steps, and the commercial page explains how stock and fixtures are handled.

How long does drying take?

It depends on what was wet and for how long. Readings at each visit show progress, and equipment comes out when the structure reads dry.
