Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Windsor, CA

Soaked carpet and pad in a Windsor bedroom, hallway or living room. We extract the water, dry what can be saved and replace what cannot.

Call (707) 221-5171

Carpet soaks up water like a sponge, and the pad underneath holds even more. The first hours decide whether carpet can be dried in place or has to be replaced. Soaked carpet and pad are worth a call to (707) 221-5171 right away, because the pad is what dries slowest.

This page covers carpet and pad. Hard flooring is on other pages, and how contaminated the water was matters as much as how much of it there was.

Soaked carpet: what to do right now

  1. Stop the source and remove standing water with a wet vacuum or towels.
  2. Lift furniture and put foil or plastic under furniture legs so wood stain does not bleed into the carpet.
  3. Open windows if weather allows, and run fans only if the carpet is clean and nothing is sewage related.
  4. Do not walk on wet carpet more than needed because it pushes water into the pad.
  5. Photograph the area and call dispatch for extraction and moisture readings.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Carpet fibers are often synthetic and tolerate water well. The pad is the weak link, because it is foam or fiber that holds water and can stay wet for days. If the water was clean and the carpet is extracted within a day or two, carpet is often dried in place and the pad may be replaced.

If the water was contaminated, such as from a toilet overflow with waste, a washer drain or flood water, the carpet and pad are usually removed. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard guides these decisions, and a written scope explains the choice.

Heat and humidity make clean water turn worse over time. The earlier extraction happens, the more choices you have.

How wet carpet is dried

The crew extracts water with a truck-mounted or portable unit, then lifts a corner of the carpet to remove the pad. Carpet is propped up with air movers blowing under it, and dehumidifiers run to pull moisture out of the room. The subfloor is checked with moisture meters and dried before carpet goes back.

If carpet is removed, floors underneath may need cleaning and drying as well. See structural drying for how the equipment is chosen and monitored.

Mold and odor concerns

Wet carpet that stays damp can develop a musty odor, and persistent moisture can support mold growth. That is why the pad and subfloor are dried and checked, not just the carpet surface. This site does not offer mold testing or remediation, so if you suspect mold, ask a qualified professional and your physician for health questions.

Questions about carpet water damage

How soon should I call about soaked carpet?

Right away. Carpet pad holds water for days, and dispatch is available at all hours to arrange extraction.

Should I pull up the carpet myself?

You can lift an edge to check, but do not remove carpet if the water may be contaminated. A crew will do it with the right equipment and protection.

How long does wet carpet take to dry?

Usually a few days with extraction, air movers and dehumidifiers. Readings decide.

Does insurance pay for carpet replacement?

Carpet from a sudden leak is frequently covered. The cause and your contents limits decide, so read the policy.

Will carpet smell afterward?

If it was dried quickly and the pad was handled properly, odor is unlikely. Persistent odor is a reason to check for hidden moisture.
