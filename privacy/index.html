Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy explains how Windsor Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website, call our phone line or email us.

Information you give us

This website has no forms. Information reaches us only when you call (707) 221-5171 or email contact@windsorwaterdamagepros.com. If you call or email, we may collect your name, phone number, address and details about the water damage.

We use that information to respond to your request, provide service, document the job and communicate with you.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers or choose email for non-urgent matters.

Website visits and third parties

Our pages include embedded Google Maps. When a map loads, your browser may contact Google, which may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control that collection.

Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, for security and operation.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share job information with your insurer, adjuster or contractors involved in your project, with your permission or as needed to do the work. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep job records as long as needed for service, documentation and legal purposes. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what information we hold about you or ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal and business needs. Write to contact@windsorwaterdamagepros.com or call (707) 221-5171. Our address is 9080 Brooks Rd, Windsor, CA 95492.

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.

Our Sonoma County coverage area

Windsor sits at the center of the area we serve, with Fulton, Healdsburg, Forestville, Santa Rosa and Geyserville around it.
