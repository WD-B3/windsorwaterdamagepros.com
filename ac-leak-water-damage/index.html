AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Windsor, CA

An air conditioner drain line or air handler has leaked into your Windsor ceiling, closet or wall. We dry what got wet and plan the repair.

Call (707) 221-5171

An air conditioner leak is condensation gone wrong. As an AC cools air, it pulls water out of it, and that water is supposed to leave the house through a drain line. When the line clogs, the pan overflows or a coil freezes and melts, water ends up in the ceiling, closet or wall. Dispatch at (707) 221-5171 handles overflow calls from air conditioners and condensate lines.

This page covers the water damage, not the air conditioner. An HVAC technician fixes the drain line, pan or coil. We dry the building materials that got wet and plan the repair.

What to do when an AC is leaking inside

  1. Turn the thermostat to off so the system stops creating condensate.
  2. Place a bucket or towels under any drip, and move belongings away from the area.
  3. If the air handler is in an attic, do not climb in alone on a hot day and do not step between joists. Call an HVAC technician and tell dispatch where the unit is.
  4. Photograph the stain, the unit and any water in the drain pan.
  5. Call an HVAC technician for the repair and call dispatch for drying.

Why AC units leak

The most common cause is a clogged condensate drain line, where algae and debris build up and stop the flow. Others are a cracked drain pan, a loose connection, a dirty filter that makes the coil freeze and then thaw, and a failed float switch that was supposed to shut the system off.

Because an air handler often sits in an attic or a closet, the water finds its way into insulation, a ceiling or a wall before anyone sees it. A small drip that lasts through a long summer can soak a ceiling more than a single burst pipe would.

How AC water damage is dried

Crews check the ceiling, attic insulation, framing and walls around the unit with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera. Wet insulation is removed, soft drywall is cut out and firm materials are dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

AC condensate is not sewage, but it can carry dust, algae and slime from the drain line, so it is cleaned up rather than left to dry in place. If the ceiling has collapsed or is stained brown, the ceiling repair page describes how that part is repaired.

AC leak versus other attic leaks

CluePoints toward
Stain appears in hot weather with no rainAC drain line or pan
Stain appears only after rainRoof leak
Stain below a bathroomPlumbing or shower pan
Water in the drain pan or rust on the unitAC condensate problem

A technician can confirm the source. Matching the pattern to the cause saves a lot of guessing before anyone opens a ceiling.

Preventing a repeat

Have the drain line cleared as part of regular HVAC service, change filters on schedule and ask the technician about a float switch that shuts the system off when the pan fills. In an attic unit, a secondary drain pan under the air handler gives an extra layer of protection.

Questions about AC leak damage

Can I call about an AC overflow on a hot weekend?

Yes. Switch the unit off, then call (707) 221-5171 and say where the water is showing. Dispatch answers all day and night, summer included.

Who fixes the AC?

An HVAC technician repairs the drain line, pan or coil. We dry the building materials and plan repairs to the ceiling or wall.

Is AC water damage covered by insurance?

Sudden leaks are often covered, while gradual leaks from poor maintenance often are not. Ask your insurer and keep records of the leak.

Can AC water cause mold?

Persistent moisture can support mold growth, which is why drying and fixing the source quickly matters. This site does not provide mold testing or remediation.

Why is my ceiling stained when the AC is on?

Condensate may be overflowing in the attic. A technician can check the drain line and pan, and moisture readings show how far the water traveled.
