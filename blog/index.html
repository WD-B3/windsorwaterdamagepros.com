Water Damage Guides for Windsor, CA Homeowners

Plain-language answers to the questions people ask before, during and after a water loss.

Call (707) 221-5171

These guides cover the questions that come up around a water loss, including insurance, claims, hidden leaks and what to throw away. They are general information and do not replace your policy or professional advice.

If water is spreading right now, skip the reading and call (707) 221-5171. Dispatch answers around the clock. For services, see our services page.

  • Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

    Sudden damage, gradual leaks and flood are treated differently. This guide explains the split and what to ask your insurer.

  • Water damage insurance claims: documentation and steps

    What to photograph, what to save and how the California claim timeline works once you report a loss.

  • Toilet leak water damage

    Supply line leaks, wax ring failures and overflows are different problems. Learn which one you have and what to do.

  • Should you file a claim for a small water leak?

    A small leak can cost less than your deductible. Here is how to weigh the repair, the claim and the risk of waiting.

  • Water damage categories 1, 2 and 3 explained

    Clean, gray and black water are treated differently. Learn how the categories work and why time matters.

  • How to tell if water damage is old or new

    Stains and soft spots can be old, active or both. Learn the clues and when a moisture reading settles the question.

  • How to find a hidden water leak in your house

    Meter tests, bill checks and a few room-by-room signs can point to a hidden leak before the damage shows.

  • How to prevent water damage when you leave home

    A ten-minute checklist covers the main valve, hoses, the water heater and who has a key while you are gone.

  • What to ask a restoration company before you hire

    Questions about license, scope, equipment, documentation and pricing help you compare companies calmly.

  • What you can dry yourself and what needs a professional

    A small clean spill is a towel job. Larger, hidden or contaminated water needs equipment. Here is where the line sits.

  • What to keep, clean or throw away after water damage

    Water type and material decide what survives. Here is a sorting guide that protects your health and your claim.

  • How to save photos and important documents after water damage

    Wet paper and photos can often be saved if you act early. Here are the steps, and where to get replacements.

  • What water damage does to electronics and when to unplug

    Cut power safely, do not power on wet devices and learn which items can be saved. Safety steps come first.

  • Water damage restoration vs remediation

    The terms overlap. This guide explains how each is used and what to look for in a scope of work.

  • What a water damage restoration estimate should include

    A clear estimate names rooms, equipment, days and exclusions. Use this checklist before you sign.
