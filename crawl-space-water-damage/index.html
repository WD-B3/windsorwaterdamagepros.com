Crawl Space Water Damage Cleanup in Windsor, CA

Water under your Windsor home soaks insulation, joists and vapor barriers. We pump it out, dry the framing and report what we find.

Call (707) 221-5171

A crawl space is the void under a raised floor, and it hides water well. Standing water, wet insulation and damp soil can go unnoticed until floors feel soft or a musty smell rises into the house. Water under the house is reported to dispatch at (707) 221-5171, where we ask a few questions about the access hatch and the depth.

This page covers the under-floor space: the vapor barrier, the insulation, the joists and the subfloor. Rising water from creeks or storms is covered on the flood page, and a basement is a different space with its own page.

Signs of water under your house

Sagging insulation hanging between joists, damp soil, standing water, a musty smell in the house and floors that feel soft or bouncy are signs. In winter, you may hear a sump pump running if one is installed. A visible waterline on piers or on the foundation wall shows how high water has risen before.

Crawl space safety

Do not enter a crawl space with standing water if wiring or ductwork runs through it. Water, electricity and a tight space are a dangerous combination. If you must look, use a flashlight from the access point and leave entry to professionals. Call 911 for any electrical concern.

Why crawl spaces get wet

Common causes are surface runoff and saturated soil in the wet season, a failed or missing vapor barrier, plumbing leaks above or inside the space, blocked foundation vents or drains and a high seasonal water table. A wet winter shows up fast under a house with poor drainage.

Ask a licensed plumber and a drainage contractor to find the source before the restoration work is finished. Drying a crawl space without fixing the source usually means it floods again.

How a wet crawl space is cleaned and dried

Crews pump out standing water, remove wet insulation, check joists and subfloor with moisture meters and set air movers and dehumidifiers that fit the tight space. Damp soil is dried as far as possible, and a vapor barrier may be replaced or repaired.

Wood framing is dense and can usually be dried when caught in time. Soaked fiberglass insulation sags, loses value and is generally removed. The drying process is explained on the structural drying page, and standing water starts with extraction.

What a restoration crew does and does not do

TaskWho handles it
Pump out water, remove wet insulation, dry framingRestoration crew
Fix leaking pipes under the houseLicensed plumber
Regrade the lot, install drains or a sump systemDrainage or foundation contractor

Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps entering, ask a qualified contractor about drainage, grading or a sump system, and ask for their license number so you can verify it with the Contractors State License Board.

Flood zones and crawl spaces

Floodplain rules can limit below-grade crawl spaces in some mapped zones, with conditions on drainage. If your home is in one, ask Windsor town staff what applies before you change the space.

Questions about crawl space water damage

Can you look at a wet crawl space after a storm?

Call dispatch at (707) 221-5171 and describe what you see from the access hatch. Do not climb in if water is deep or near wiring.

Can I go in and look myself?

Only if no water is present near wiring or ducts, and you have a flashlight and a way out. For deep water or unknown conditions, wait for professionals.

Does homeowners insurance cover a wet crawl space?

It depends on the cause. Sudden pipe leaks are often covered, while groundwater and flood are usually excluded. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Will you waterproof the crawl space?

No. Restoration dries and cleans. If water keeps coming in, the fix belongs to a drainage specialist.

How long does a crawl space take to dry?

Anywhere from several days to longer, depending on soil moisture and ventilation. Moisture readings decide when it is done.
