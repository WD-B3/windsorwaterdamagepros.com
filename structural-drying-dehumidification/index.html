Structural Drying and Dehumidification in Windsor, CA

After the water is out, the structure still holds moisture. We map it, dry it with controlled equipment and log readings until it is dry.

Call (707) 221-5171

Wall still damp after a leak, a musty smell that will not leave, a floor that feels cool and soft. These are what people notice when a structure is still wet. Structural drying is how Windsor Water Damage Pros finds that moisture and removes it with air movers, dehumidifiers and moisture readings, and the dispatch line at (707) 221-5171 is open 24 hours a day.

Structural drying is the core of water mitigation. Mitigation means limiting damage after a water loss, and for most homes the most important part is getting framing, subfloor, drywall and insulation back to a dry standard before they warp, rot or support mold growth.

Wall still damp or a musty smell after a leak?

Walls and floors can look dry while holding moisture inside. Drywall wicks water up from the floor, insulation holds it in cavities and wood subfloors release it slowly. People often notice a leftover smell, paint that bubbles or baseboard that swells weeks after a leak, and they ask how long drying should take.

The answer depends on the materials, the amount of water, the time since the loss and the equipment. Readings, not a calendar, tell when a structure is dry.

How structural drying works

  1. Moisture mapping: meters and a thermal imaging camera find wet areas and set a starting record.
  2. Equipment placement: air movers move air across wet surfaces, and LGR dehumidifiers pull water vapor from the air.
  3. Access: small holes or removed baseboards let air reach wall cavities and flooring edges.
  4. Daily monitoring: readings are repeated and equipment is adjusted.
  5. Drying goal: materials are brought down to a dry standard before equipment is removed.

This is the approach laid out in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. Ask any company whether its crews follow it and how they will share your daily readings.

Water mitigation in plain words

Water mitigation means the steps taken to stop damage from getting worse. That includes shutting off the source, removing water, protecting contents, removing materials that cannot be dried and drying what can. It is different from rebuilding, which comes afterward and replaces what was lost.

Mitigation is time sensitive. The sooner a structure is opened and dried, the more of it can usually be saved. Water that sits for days leaves more to replace.

What drying equipment does

EquipmentWhat it does
Moisture meterReads moisture in drywall, wood and subfloor
Thermal imaging cameraShows temperature differences that reveal wet areas
Air moverMoves air across wet surfaces to speed evaporation
LGR dehumidifierPulls water vapor out of the air so materials keep releasing moisture
Extraction unitRemoves liquid water before drying begins, see water extraction

Equipment is placed by the structure, not by a formula. A closet and a great room need different setups, and the equipment list changes as the readings change.

How long drying takes

A lightly wet room may dry in a few days, while thick materials, hardwood and slabs take longer. We do not promise a fixed number of days because it would not be honest. What we can promise is that readings will be recorded and shared so you can see progress.

Equipment will be loud, and dehumidifiers add heat to the room. Ask what to expect so you can plan around it. If you rent your home, tell the landlord and keep records for the insurer.

Questions about structural drying

Can drying start the same night as the leak?

Extraction and equipment setup can begin when a crew is available, and dispatch is reachable at any hour. Drying continues over days until readings reach the target.

Do I need to leave the house during drying?

Usually not, but the equipment is loud. For sewage or heavy damage, crews may recommend staying elsewhere.

Can I just run my own fans and a dehumidifier?

For a small, clean spill caught quickly, maybe. For anything soaked into walls or floors, home equipment lacks the capacity and you will not have readings to prove it is dry.

Will drying prevent mold?

Drying removes the moisture that mold needs, but no company can guarantee mold will never grow. Fix the source and dry quickly.

Does the crew return to check readings?

Yes. Readings are taken at each visit until materials meet the dry standard.
