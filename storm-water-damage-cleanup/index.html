Emergency Storm Water Damage Cleanup in Windsor, CA

Storm rain has come in through a roof, window, wall or door in your Windsor home. We dry the structure and document the damage for your insurer.

Call (707) 221-5171

Storm water damage is what happens when wind and rain defeat the building envelope. Shingles lift, flashing fails, a window leaks or a tree limb opens the roof, and water gets into attics, walls and ceilings. During and after storms, calls to (707) 221-5171 reach dispatch, though volume can be high.

This page covers water that enters through the building from above and the sides during a storm. Water that rises from the ground or a creek is on the flood page, and a slow roof leak without a storm event is covered with ceiling damage.

When storm water is an emergency

If water is coming in during a storm, ceilings are bulging or a roof section has failed, call right away. During a regional storm many homes need help at the same time, so tell dispatch what is wet, where the water is entering and whether you can safely tarp or cover the opening. Work follows once crews can reach you safely.

Storm hazards

Stay away from downed power lines and anything touching them, and call 911 and the utility. Do not climb onto a wet roof. Keep out of rooms with bulging ceilings. If a tree has fallen on the house, leave until the structure has been checked.

  1. Move people to a safe room and keep away from wet ceilings and electrical hazards.
  2. Place buckets and towels, and move belongings away from drips.
  3. Photograph the damage as soon as it is safe, including exterior damage.
  4. Cover openings with plastic sheeting or a tarp if you can do so from the ground or a safe position.
  5. Call dispatch and your insurer.

Windsor storms and what the record shows

In January 2023 a run of storms led the Town of Windsor to proclaim a local emergency. A stretch of weather like that exposes every weak flashing, clogged gutter and worn roof.

The practical lesson is to inspect the roof, gutters and windows before the wet season, and to keep photos of the home in good condition for your insurer. After a storm, check the attic when it is safe, because wet insulation can be the first sign of damage.

How storm water damage is cleaned up

Crews remove standing water, take out saturated insulation and check ceilings, walls and floors with moisture meters and thermal imaging. Water that arrives through the roof soaks insulation first, so attic checks matter even when the stain is in a downstairs room.

After mitigation, a roofer repairs the roof and the restoration crew dries and prepares the structure for repair. Temporary covers are common, and insurers usually want to see that you limited further loss.

Many storms cause both, which is why a photo record of how the water got in is useful. Tell your insurer exactly what you saw.

Permits and licensed work after a storm

After a storm, out-of-area contractors often knock on doors. Check the license on the CSLB website, ask for a written scope and read the contract before you sign. The Town of Windsor building counter can tell you if the repair needs a permit.

Questions about storm water damage

Will anyone answer during a winter storm?

Yes, dispatch answers throughout storms. Many homes call at once, so crews are sent as they free up, and we never promise a time.

Is storm damage covered by insurance?

Wind and rain damage through the roof is often covered under a homeowners policy, while rising water is usually excluded. Read your policy and ask your insurer.

Can you tarp my roof?

Roof work is a roofer's job. We focus on water removal and drying, and can recommend you call a licensed roofer for the temporary cover or repair.

How do I protect my claim?

Photograph before and after, keep receipts for emergency steps and keep a log of calls with your insurer, as the California Department of Insurance advises.

Should I wait until the rain stops?

Call now. You can make protective steps safe, and dispatch can plan the visit when conditions allow.
