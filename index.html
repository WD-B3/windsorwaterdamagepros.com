Our process

How a water damage job runs

  1. 1

    Call and triage

    You describe the water and the source. Dispatch tells you what to do about the valve, the power and the people in the house.

  2. 2

    Extract and protect

    Crews remove standing water with extraction units and pumps, and move or protect belongings in the way.

  3. 3

    Measure and plan

    Moisture meters and thermal imaging map the wet areas. The plan names the equipment and the materials to remove.

  4. 4

    Dry the structure

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run while we log readings until the materials reach their dry targets.

  5. 5

    Document and hand off

    You get a written record for your insurer and a clear line between drying and repairs.

What happens in the first 24 to 72 hours

Water does most of its damage in the first three days. In the first hours, it spreads along floors, wicks into drywall and soaks carpet pad. By the end of the first day, wood begins to swell and finishes start to fail. By the third day, materials that could have been dried may need to be replaced.

Time since the waterWhat is usually happeningWhat helps most
First hoursWater spreads and wicks into porous materialsStop the source, extract, call
Up to 24 hoursCarpet pad, drywall and wood absorb waterStart drying and take readings
24 to 48 hoursWood swells, finishes loosen, odors startRun air movers and dehumidifiers continuously
48 to 72 hoursMaterials begin to degrade or stay wet insideCheck cavities and replace what cannot dry

These are general patterns, not guarantees. Every house, material and water source is different. The point is that the earlier drying starts, the more of the house you keep. Read about the first visit on the water extraction page.

Emergency water damage: what to do before the crew arrives

  1. Stop the source. Close a fixture valve or the main valve.
  2. Stay out of rooms where water is near electrical equipment.
  3. Call (707) 221-5171 and describe the water.
  4. Lift small items and furniture off wet floors.
  5. Photograph the damage before you move things.
  6. Report the loss to your insurer.

If the water came from a toilet, a drain or outside, keep children and pets away and treat it as contaminated. We explain the categories on the sewage page and the flood damage page.

Water damage repair in Windsor: dry first, then rebuild

Water damage repair has two parts. Restoration dries and cleans what the water wet. Repair puts finishes back, such as drywall, paint, flooring and cabinets. Doing the second before the first is finished traps moisture in the wall and brings the problem back.

We handle the drying and the documentation, and we tell you honestly where drying ends and rebuilding begins. For wet walls, see the wall and drywall page. For floors, see the hardwood floor page.

What water damage restoration costs and what drives it

We do not publish a flat price, because the same room can be a two-hour job or a week-long one. Cost depends on how much water there is, how long it sat, what it touched and how many square feet need drying. Contaminated water adds steps, and hidden water adds time.

Insurance and water damage claims

Sudden damage is often covered. Flood usually is not.

Coverage usually turns on whether the water was sudden or slow, and on whether it came from a pipe or from outside. A homeowners policy often responds to the first, rarely to a slow seep and usually not to flood. Read yours and talk to your insurer. Questions about water damage in Windsor

Do you handle emergency water damage in Windsor at night?

Yes. Nights, weekends and holidays are covered by the same dispatch line, (707) 221-5171. Crews go as they free up, and we do not quote arrival times.

How fast does water damage get worse?

Damage spreads in the first hours and gets harder to reverse over the first one to three days. Starting extraction and drying early protects more of the house.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

A pipe that bursts is often covered, a slow seep often is not and flood is usually a separate policy. Check yours and ask your insurer.

What does water damage restoration cost?

It depends on the amount of water, how long it sat, the materials involved and the drying time. We explain the scope and price before work starts.
