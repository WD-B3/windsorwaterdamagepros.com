Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Windsor, CA

Water under the sink, behind the dishwasher or in the cabinets of your Windsor kitchen. We dry cabinet bases, flooring and walls.

Call (707) 221-5171

A kitchen holds supply lines, drain lines, a dishwasher, a refrigerator water line and often a sink that has leaked for months. Water damage in the kitchen usually shows up as swollen cabinet bases, soft toe kicks, warped flooring or a musty smell under the sink. Kitchen leaks go to dispatch at (707) 221-5171, where we ask about the sink, the dishwasher and the floor.

This page covers cabinets, countertops, the sink area and the floor under them. The appliance failure itself is covered on the appliance page, and a pipe that burst in a wall is on the burst pipe page.

Water under the kitchen sink

The area under a sink is a common leak site because it holds the supply stops, the trap and the drain connections. A slow drip can saturate the cabinet floor, the wall behind it and the subfloor, and it often goes unnoticed because stored items hide it. If you notice swelling, stains or a musty smell, empty the cabinet and look for moisture.

  1. Close the angle stop valves under the sink or the main shutoff if water is running.
  2. Empty the cabinet and photograph the damage.
  3. Dry any standing water with towels.
  4. Call a plumber for the leak and dispatch for drying.

What water does to cabinets

Cabinet materialWhat water doesLikely result
Solid woodAbsorbs and can swell, but dries wellOften dried in place
Plywood boxesDelaminate if soaked for longDried if caught early, replaced if layers separate
Particleboard or MDFSwells and crumblesUsually replaced once swollen
Laminate or thermofoil facesPeel at edgesReplaced if the base has swollen

Cabinet bases that stay wet for days usually have to be replaced, while those caught early can sometimes be dried through the toe kick opening. Moisture readings show which.

How kitchen water damage is dried

The crew removes toe kicks so air can reach the cabinet base, checks the flooring and subfloor and uses a moisture meter and thermal imaging to follow water behind the dishwasher and under cabinets. Air movers and dehumidifiers are placed to dry the cabinet box, subfloor and wall.

Flooring is handled by type. Tile and sheet vinyl may stay, while laminate and some engineered flooring absorb water at the edges and may be removed. If water went below the kitchen into a basement or crawl space, the drying plan extends there, as covered on the structural drying page.

Hidden water behind appliances

Dishwashers and refrigerators sit against walls, and their supply lines are out of sight. If the floor in front of a dishwasher is spongy or you see a stain on the ceiling below the kitchen, water may have been traveling for a while. The appliance leak page covers the failures behind the units, and the ceiling page covers what shows up below.

Counters, backsplash and finishes

Stone and solid surface counters are not damaged by water, but they sit on cabinet bases that are. Laminate counters with particleboard cores can swell at seams. Backsplash tile can loosen if the wall behind it is wet. A restoration plan should say which finishes are removed to dry what is behind them and who reinstalls them.

Questions about kitchen water damage

What if the sink cabinet floods late at night?

Close the angle stops under the sink or the main valve and call. Dispatch is open 24 hours and will explain what to empty and photograph first.

Can swollen cabinets be saved?

Plywood or solid wood boxes often dry well if caught early. Particleboard that has swollen usually cannot be restored.

Is a slow sink leak covered by insurance?

A long, slow leak is often excluded as gradual damage, while a sudden failure is often covered. Check your policy.

Do I need to remove my countertops?

Not always. Many jobs let the crew dry under cabinets through toe kick openings, but the plan depends on readings.

Who fixes the leak?

A plumber. We dry and plan repairs to what the water damaged.
