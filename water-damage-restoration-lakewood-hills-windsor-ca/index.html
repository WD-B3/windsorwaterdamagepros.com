Water Damage Restoration in Lakewood Hills, Windsor, CA

Water inside a Lakewood Hills home or townhouse? Call dispatch and tell us whether it came from a pipe, an appliance or the outside.

  • 95492
Call (707) 221-5171

Lakewood Hills residents who find water in a house or townhome can call (707) 221-5171 and describe what is wet. We are based in Windsor, and Lakewood Hills is a Windsor community off Lakewood Drive that we cover.

It is a gated community with two private lakes. A March 2025 notice from the North Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board lists the community at 9300 Lakewood Drive and names the Lakewood Hills Homeowners Association as owner of North Lake and South Lake.

Living beside two private lakes

The Water Board notice describes two man-made lakes joined by a drainage canal. They fill mostly with rain and are topped up in the dry season from a private well. In heavy rain, overflow can leave the lakes through drain structures into the Town storm drain system and then to East Windsor Creek and a tributary of it.

That is a description of how the lake system is permitted to discharge. It does not say that homes in Lakewood Hills flood, and we make no such claim. It does explain why the association is the right first call when the question is about the lakes, their drains or the grounds.

Houses, townhomes and shared walls

Local real estate guides describe Lakewood Hills as developed in the late 1980s with single-family homes on larger lots and townhouse-style units. We use that only as a general picture, since the guides are not an official record. Where homes share a wall, one unit's leak can reach the next, so tell dispatch if the water crossed a boundary.

Ask your association which parts of a building it insures and which are yours. That answer changes who files a claim and for what.

Services residents ask about

A heavy rain can push water through a threshold or a garage door, and the storm water page covers that kind of cleanup. A leak between floors is a ceiling repair job, and an overflowing toilet that has spread dirty water is handled under sewage backup cleanup.

Wet carpet, baseboards and drywall come out or get dried in place depending on the water and the time. We explain the decision room by room, and you keep a written scope.

First steps while you wait

  1. Stop the source if you can do so safely, using the fixture shutoff or the main valve.
  2. Keep everyone away from standing water near outlets, cords and appliances.
  3. Move small valuables and papers to a dry room.
  4. Photograph the damage before you clean.
  5. Call (707) 221-5171 and describe the source and how far the water has spread.

Do not use a household vacuum on standing water and do not walk through water that may be dirty. If there is any danger to people, call 911 first.

Flood status and insurance

Whether your Lakewood Hills address sits in a mapped flood zone is something we did not verify. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center answers it for one address at a time, and the association may know more about drainage on the grounds.

Standard homeowner policies and flood policies treat water differently, and association policies add another layer. Read yours, ask what applies to rainwater and sudden pipe failures, and keep records of what we find.

Questions from Lakewood Hills residents

Is emergency water removal available in Lakewood Hills?

Yes. Ring (707) 221-5171 whatever the hour and name your street inside the gate. A crew is assigned as one is free, and no arrival time is quoted.

Do the lakes put my home at risk of flooding?

We cannot say. The lakes are described in a regulator notice as rain-fed with controlled overflow, and we have not verified any home's flood status. Check FEMA's map for your address and ask the association about its drainage.

Who handles water coming from a shared wall?

It depends on where the water started and on your association rules. Document it, notify the association and both insurers, and we can dry your side.

Can you get through the gate?

Give dispatch the gate instructions or arrange a contact who can let a crew in. That avoids delay on arrival.

Will you tell me whether insurance pays?

No. Photos, readings and a written scope come from us, and the decision on coverage belongs to the insurer under your policy.
