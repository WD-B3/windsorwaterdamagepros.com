Water damage restoration in Healdsburg starts with one call to (707) 221-5171. Windsor Water Damage Pros is based about seven miles southeast of Healdsburg along US 101, and dispatch answers the emergency line around the clock.

Healdsburg is an incorporated city in Sonoma County, incorporated on February 20, 1867, with 11,340 residents at the 2020 census. It sits on the Russian River, its ZIP code is 95448, and US 101 reached the city in 1964.

Around the Plaza and downtown Healdsburg

Healdsburg is built around a central plaza that founder Harmon Heald laid out in 1857, and the blocks around it hold shops, restaurants and tasting rooms. Water in a commercial space like that affects neighbors, inventory and operating days, so the first call should come as soon as the leak is found.

The commercial page shows how a shop or tasting room loss is scoped around operating hours. For a wet restaurant or retail floor, the water extraction page covers the first steps.

Rivers, creeks and the flood question

The Russian River runs along the city, and Foss Creek flows through it and joins Dry Creek near the US 101 interchange in central Healdsburg. If water has come up from the ground or over a bank, treat it as flood water, which is contaminated and usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy.

Before you buy, rebuild or file a claim, put your address into the FEMA flood map lookup. The City of Healdsburg website also has a floodplain mapping section under stormwater, and the City is at 401 Grove Street if you want to ask in person. Our flood damage page covers cleanup in detail.

When water is spreading in Healdsburg

A supply line, a dishwasher hose or a water heater can fail at any hour. Close the shutoff if you can reach it, then call. Dispatch will ask where the water is, how much there is and whether anything electrical is wet.

  1. Find the shutoff and close it
  2. Photograph the room before moving things
  3. Call (707) 221-5171
  4. Keep anyone with health concerns out of the wet area

When the crew arrives, they extract standing water, take moisture readings and set drying equipment. The water heater leak page has the same sequence for a failed tank.

If water is still spreading: your first ten minutes

Valve first

Shut the fixture valve under the sink or toilet, or the main valve where the line enters the house.

Power only if dry

Shut off circuits to wet rooms when the panel can be reached from dry floor.

Raise belongings

Move electronics, papers and small furniture off the floor. Rugs come up so they do not stain the floor.

Protect people

Keep kids and pets away from sewage, drain or river water. Wear boots if you must cross a wet floor.

Record it

A short video of the room, walls and ceiling helps an adjuster later.

Dial dispatch

Call the 24/7 line at (707) 221-5171. A person answers.

Insurance and permits

Whether a loss counts as sudden or gradual often decides the claim, and river water is a separate flood question. Read your policy and talk to your insurer. We hand you readings, photos and a written scope for the adjuster.

Inside the city limits, building questions go to the City of Healdsburg. Outside them, in the county, they go to Permit Sonoma. Ask which applies to your address before you start repairs.
