Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Windsor, CA

A stained, sagging or dripping ceiling in your Windsor home. We find where the water came from, dry the ceiling and attic space, and plan the repair.

Call (707) 221-5171

A ceiling stain is rarely the whole story. Water comes from somewhere above, and by the time a ceiling shows a brown ring, the insulation and framing above it have usually been wet for a while. Dripping, bulging or collapsed ceilings are handled through (707) 221-5171, and the same page covers roof leaks that feed them.

This page covers the finished ceiling and whatever sits above it, whether the water came from a roof, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an air conditioner in the attic. A ceiling can hold a surprising amount of water in the drywall and insulation, then drop all at once.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling that is bulging, dripping steadily or sagging in the middle is an emergency. Water weighs about eight pounds a gallon, and wet drywall and insulation can fall without warning. Call dispatch and stay out of the room below the damage.

Before the ceiling lets go

Move people and pets out of the room below a bulging ceiling. If water is running through a light fixture, do not use the switch. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it without standing in water, and call 911 or the utility for any electrical concern. A small hole drilled into the lowest part of a bulge can release trapped water, but only a professional should do that safely.

  1. Place a bucket under any drip and move furniture and electronics away.
  2. Shut off the water to the room above if a pipe is the suspected source.
  3. Photograph the stain, its size and any change over the next hours.
  4. Call dispatch and describe whether it rains, whether the stain grows only when it rains and whether a bathroom or air conditioner is overhead.

Finding where the water came from

A stain that grows only after rain points to the roof, a stain below a bathroom or laundry points to supply lines, drains or a shower pan, and a stain that appears in hot weather may point to an air conditioner condensate line in the attic. Crews follow the pattern with moisture meters and thermal imaging, then inspect the attic or the space above.

A restoration crew dries and repairs what the water damaged, but roof repair is roofing work and plumbing repair is plumbing work. Our job is to make sure the ceiling is dry and the damaged material is replaced after the source is fixed. If a leak is still active, the best next call may also be a roofer or plumber.

Roof leaks that damage ceilings

Roof leaks reach a ceiling slowly. Water enters at a flashing, a worn shingle or a vent boot, runs along the decking, soaks insulation and finally stains the drywall far from the entry point. Windsor's wet season is concentrated in winter, and a worn flashing or cracked vent boot often shows itself in the first heavy storm of the season.

If the roof is the source, ask a roofer to inspect it as soon as the weather allows and ask your insurer whether storm damage is covered. Covering the opening from a safe position is a sensible stopgap, and many policies expect some protective effort. A restoration crew can dry the attic and ceiling in the meantime.

How ceiling water damage is dried and repaired

Wet insulation is removed because it holds water and loses its value when soaked. Drywall that is sagging, stained through or soft is cut out, while firm drywall may dry in place with air movers and dehumidifiers. Moisture readings confirm that framing in the attic is dry before anything is closed up.

Repair then follows in order. New drywall is hung, taped and textured, the ceiling is primed so the stain does not bleed through and paint is matched. Painting over a stain without drying it rarely works, because a damp ceiling keeps pulling water back to the surface.

Questions about ceiling water damage

What should I do about a bulging ceiling at 2 a.m.?

Keep out from under it, place a bucket if you can do so safely and call (707) 221-5171. Do not poke the bulge, because it may hold a lot of water.

Can a stained ceiling just be painted over?

Only after the ceiling is dry and the source is fixed. Paint over a damp stain usually bleeds through again, and trapped moisture can damage drywall and insulation.

Does a roof leak count as ceiling water damage?

On this page, yes. The roof is the source and the ceiling is where the damage shows. A roofer repairs the roof and we dry and repair the ceiling and insulation.

What should I photograph for insurance?

Photograph the stain, the room, the attic if you can reach it safely and any wet belongings. Keep dated notes and a log of calls with your insurer.

How long does a ceiling take to dry?

Drywall can dry in a few days, but attic insulation and framing may take longer. Moisture readings decide when the ceiling can be closed.
